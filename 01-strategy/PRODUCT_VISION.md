# Sycamore product vision

This document describes the accepted G3 product and its historical refactor
scope. The staged evolution is now specified in
[the intelligence roadmap](INTELLIGENCE_PLATFORM_ROADMAP.md). Its initial
commercial hypothesis is a recurring analyst workflow: understand what changed,
trace evidence, and produce a defensible brief. The old refactor exclusions below
do not prohibit future user-authorized epochs; they describe what G3 included.

A calm, sourced view of the world's cyber, geopolitical, maritime, and military
reporting. The primary audience is readers who want geographic context and a
clear path from a headline to original reporting.

## Current local redesign

- Map and chronological feed visible together on desktop; Map/Feed tabs on mobile.
- Search and topic/severity/time filters with shareable URLs.
- Event detail with source summaries, original links, and explicit classification context.
- Boards grouped by topic and preserved static event pages.
- Briefing across the last day, week, and month, based on retained reporting.
- Honest successful-check timestamps, delayed/offline states, and clearly labeled demo mode.

Favor readable headlines, clear selection, restrained motion, and useful source
links. Remove sample markets/outlooks, scrolling ticker, watchlists, and
comparison from this version. Coverage and freshness should earn repeat visits.

Sycamore is retained as the local display name by user direction. Operation SM
remains the related content project, but social cross-promotion and public brand
rollout are separate tasks. No new accounts, alerts, payments, providers,
community features, or public deployment are included in this refactor.
