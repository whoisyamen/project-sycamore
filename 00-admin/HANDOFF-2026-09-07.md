## Animated ocean and night lights

`src/client/globe-effects.ts` now owns a globe surface shader and appearance
controls. It adds 4K NASA/GEBCO ocean depth shading, two animated wave scales,
sun reflections, coastal blending, a soft day/night boundary and NASA Black
Marble 2016 night lights. Controls: Water motion, Night lights, Time lapse (600×).
Time lapse is explicit and restores the current date on exit; reduced motion
defaults to a static surface and clock. Labels distinguish the historical composite.

Ground haze and built-in globe lighting remain off; surface shader handles
shading, and the outer atmosphere returns at low intensity (6). Runtime textures
are local under `public/data/globe/effects/`; see SOURCES.md for provenance and
reproduction. Globe initialization installs effects and teardown releases the
material, UI and frame subscription. New tests cover pause, reduced motion,
night/time-lapse state, mode switching and cleanup.
